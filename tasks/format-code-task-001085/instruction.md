The pagination primitive needs to remain a valid, controlled state machine when callers provide incomplete or changing data. Keep the existing item-window, ellipsis, accessibility-attribute, and callback APIs, but normalize the numeric inputs and keep the current page inside the range implied by the current total and page size.

`total` represents a count of records and cannot be negative; `perPage` must be a positive number (invalid or non-positive assignments use 1). With no records, expose zero pages and a 0-0 range. Otherwise there is one page for each non-empty chunk, and the initial page and every later assignment are clamped to that range. Construction must not invoke `onChange`; later calls invoke it exactly once only when the effective page changes, including a correction caused by changing `total` or `perPage`.

Changing `total` or `perPage` through their returned stores must immediately recompute `items`, `start`, and `end`, preserve a still-valid page, and correct an out-of-range page. `start` and `end` are one-based record positions for non-empty pages, with `end` capped at `total`; for the empty result both are 0.

The returned navigation items must continue to expose unique page numbers from 1 through the current page count, exactly one selected page, disabled first/previous controls at the lower edge and disabled next/last controls at the upper edge, and ellipsis items only as disabled gaps with a null page. Existing options for hiding controls, boundary pages, sibling pages, labels, and the `pageAttrs` accessibility state remain compatible.
