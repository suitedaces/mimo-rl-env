DatePicker's keyboard support currently stops at moving one calendar day or week, and it refuses to move at all when the immediate destination is disabled. This makes keyboard navigation slow around unavailable dates and leaves common calendar-grid commands unsupported. Extend the DatePicker's key-release navigation contract so focus can move predictably without selecting a date.

For the arrow keys, Left and Right move by one calendar day and Up and Down move by seven calendar days. If that destination is disabled by `disableSpecificDates`, continue in the same direction until the next enabled date; consecutive disabled dates must be skipped in either direction. Do not move past `disableDatesBefore` or `disableDatesAfter`: at either range boundary focus stays on the current day, and neither `onChange` nor `onMonthChange` fires. Arrow-key focus movement itself must not call `onChange`.

Add the standard larger calendar movements. Home focuses the first day of the current calendar week and End focuses the last, using the component's `weekStartsOn` value. PageUp and PageDown move to the same day in the previous or next month. Holding Shift with PageUp or PageDown instead moves to the same month and day in the previous or next year. When the target month does not contain that day, clamp to its last day; for example January 31 moves to February 28, and February 29 moves to February 28 in a non-leap year.

Keyboard focus and the controlled display must stay coordinated across month and year boundaries. When a destination is outside the month or months currently rendered, call `onMonthChange` once with its zero-based month and full year, then focus that day after the parent supplies the new display. Preserve the existing arrow-key transition across a year boundary. In `multiMonth` mode, a destination in the already-rendered second month is focused directly and must not call `onMonthChange`. The navigation behavior must work through the rendered day buttons and callbacks without changing DatePicker's controlled `month`/`year` API.
